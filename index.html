What is Gupshup Thikana?

A word that means a haven for endless tales.

Food is all about its story — the sizzle of tawa momos in clay handis, the melted cheese stretching across stone-baked crusts, the rich aroma of freshly roasted hazelnut coffee, and conversations that stretch until dawn.

‘Gupshup Thikana’ tells the extraordinary stories behind the foods we love: authentic street & cafe cuisine, served with careful attention to every detail of taste, service and atmosphere — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Gupshup Thikana is a place of gathering, of celebrations, of late-night chai, and of pleasure. Let the story begin… — The Thikana Promise
